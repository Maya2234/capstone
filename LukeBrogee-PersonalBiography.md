## Featured Project

### CardStacker
> *A smart checkout plugin optimizing consumer credit card rewards at the point of sale.*

**CardStacker** is an e-commerce checkout integration designed for merchant platforms. At checkout, shoppers can choose to pay via CardStacker to instantly maximize their reward yield.

#### How It Works
* **Real-Time Optimization:** CardStacker’s algorithm analyzes the merchant's category code (MCC) and the user's connected card portfolio to identify the card that yields the highest cash-back rate or reward points for that specific purchase.
* **Frictionless Rewards:** Eliminates the need for consumers to manually track rotating quarterly bonus categories or merchant-specific reward rules.
