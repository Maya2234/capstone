### **CardStack**
---
A payment method that will allow users to utilize one payemnt option to automatically select from pre-authorized cards and accounts to maximize benefits on a transaction
Stage one: A payment wallet to be implemented by merchants.
Stage two: A single card / credentials per user that will provide card and tap integration expanding usability to any card accepting merchant online and in person.
